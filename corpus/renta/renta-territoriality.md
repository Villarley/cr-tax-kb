# Income tax on profits (ISU), territoriality and 2026 brackets

## Territoriality
<!-- es: principio de territorialidad, renta de fuente costarricense, ingresos del exterior, trabajo hecho en Costa Rica para clientes extranjeros, rentas pasivas del extranjero -->

Costa Rica taxes only **Costa Rican-source income**. Art. 1 of the income tax law (as reformed by Ley 9635): "se entenderá por rentas, ingresos, o beneficios de fuente costarricense, los generados en el territorio nacional". Income from services is sourced where the work is performed, so a resident freelancer working from Costa Rica for a foreign client has Costa Rican-source income, even if paid abroad in USD or crypto. Purely foreign passive income (dividends, interest, gains on foreign assets) has historically been outside the scope; bills to tax it are pending (see [[crypto-assets]] and [[renta-capital]]).

## Who pays the ISU
<!-- es: quién paga impuesto sobre las utilidades, actividad lucrativa, utilidad neta, gastos deducibles, persona física y jurídica -->

The Impuesto sobre las Utilidades (ISU) applies to net profits from a lucrative activity (actividad lucrativa) carried on by individuals (persona física con actividad lucrativa) and legal entities (persona jurídica). Net profit = gross income minus deductible expenses that are useful, necessary and pertinent to produce the income and backed by electronic invoices.

## 2026 brackets: individuals with a lucrative activity (annual net income)

Source: Decreto 45333-H (La Gaceta 229, December 5, 2025), via EY.

| Annual net income (₡) | Rate |
| --- | --- |
| Up to 6,244,000 | 0% |
| 6,244,000 to 8,329,000 | 10% |
| 8,329,000 to 10,414,000 | 15% |
| 10,414,000 to 20,872,000 | 20% |
| Over 20,872,000 | 25% |

Credits for 2026: ₡20,520 per child and ₡31,080 for a spouse, per year. Note: the 0% threshold rose a lot from 2025 (₡4,094,000 reported by EY for 2025). Verify against the decree text before hard-coding it.

| Annual net income (₡) | Rate |
| --- | --- |
| Up to 5,621,000 | 5% |
| 5,621,000 to 8,433,000 | 10% |

Legal entities with gross income above ₡119,174,000 pay the **general rate of 30%** on net profit (Ley 7092; rate not in the EY alert, confirm against the law text).

## Fiscal period, annual return and partial payments
<!-- es: período fiscal enero a diciembre, declaración anual de renta 15 de marzo, formularios 101 y 102, pagos parciales junio setiembre diciembre, adelantos -->

- The fiscal period is the calendar year (January 1 to December 31).
- The annual return is due by **March 15** of the following year: TRIBU-CR form **101** (individual), **102** (legal entity), 103 (public entity). These replaced the old D-101.
- **Partial payments (pagos parciales):** three advance payments during the year, the last one due December 31 (El Observador). The usual schedule is the last business days of June, September and December, each a share of the prior year's tax. Confirm the exact computation with the accountant before automating reminders.

## Small taxpayers
<!-- es: pequeños contribuyentes, régimen simplificado en lugar de renta e IVA -->

Individuals with small retail activities can opt for the régimen de tributación simplificada, which replaces the ISU and IVA computation with quarterly filings (forms 104, 105 and 153). See [[regimen-simplificado]].
