# IVA basics under Ley 9635

## Scope (art. 1)
<!-- es: hecho generador del IVA, venta de bienes y prestación de servicios, servicios contratados a proveedores del exterior o empresas extranjeras, autoliquidación por el comprador, reverse charge, factura electrónica de compra -->

IVA applies to the sale of goods and the provision of services in Costa Rica: "Se establece un impuesto sobre el valor agregado en la venta de bienes y en la prestación de servicios". Services supplied by a provider abroad are also taxed when the recipient is an IVA taxpayer located in Costa Rica (art. 1, "cuando el destinatario sea un contribuyente del artículo 4 de esta ley y esté ubicado en el territorio de la República"). That is the reverse charge case: the Costa Rican buyer self-assesses the IVA.

In e-invoicing v4.4 the buyer documents this with a **factura electrónica de compra**, now extended to services and intangibles bought from foreign companies with no Costa Rican domicile, including the IVA (see [[facturacion-electronica-v44]]).

## Taxpayers (art. 4)
<!-- es: quién es contribuyente del IVA, inscripción obligatoria, exportadores, freelancers, régimen simplificado, actividad habitual -->

Anyone who habitually sells goods or provides services is a taxpayer. The law adds that all **exporters** and everyone in the **régimen de tributación simplificada** are taxpayers ("son contribuyentes todos los exportadores y los que se acojan al régimen de tributación simplificada"). A freelancer who only exports services must still register for IVA, issue export invoices and file the monthly return.

## Rates (arts. 10 and 11)
<!-- es: tarifas del IVA, 13% general, tarifas reducidas 4% 2% 1%, servicios de salud privados, médicos, educación privada, medicamentos, seguros, boletos aéreos, canasta básica, exento 0% -->

| Rate | Applies to (summary) |
| --- | --- |
| 13% | General rate, all taxable operations (art. 10) |
| 4% | Airline tickets to or from Costa Rica; private health services (art. 11) |
| 2% | Medicines and some production inputs; private education; personal insurance premiums, among others (art. 11) |
| 1% | Canasta básica; certain agricultural goods and inputs; certain animal feed inputs (art. 11) |
| 0% / exempt | Exports and services used abroad (art. 8), see [[iva-exports]] |

Crypto: the DGT considers services related to virtual assets, including buying and selling them and the commissions charged, subject to IVA at 13% (see [[crypto-assets]]).

## Filing and payment (art. 27)
<!-- es: declarar y pagar el IVA mensual, día 15 de cada mes, formulario 150, liquidación mensual -->

IVA is self-assessed **monthly**: taxpayers "deben liquidar el impuesto a más tardar el decimoquinto día natural de cada mes" for the previous month. In TRIBU-CR the general return is form **150** (formerly D-104). See [[tribu-cr]].

## Input credit and refunds (art. 28)
<!-- es: crédito fiscal del IVA pagado en compras, devolución del IVA, saldo a favor, aceptación de facturas, mensaje receptor, devolución expedita 75% -->

IVA paid on purchases linked to taxable or exempt-with-credit operations (such as exports) is credited against IVA charged. To claim it, the purchase must be backed by an electronic invoice issued to the taxpayer and accepted (mensaje receptor). Taxpayers whose credit-eligible operations (including some exports) generate credits above 75% may request an expedited refund (art. 28). Simplified-regime taxpayers cannot use input credits (art. 41).

## Credit sales and the payment receipt
<!-- es: ventas a crédito, pagar el IVA cuando el cliente paga, recibo electrónico de pago, plazo de 90 días, pymes y profesionales independientes -->

Under v4.4, independent professionals and small and medium businesses that sell on credit can defer paying the IVA until they are paid (credit terms up to 90 days), documenting the payment with the new **recibo electrónico de pago** (Deloitte summary of MH-DGT-RES-0027-2024). Large taxpayers are excluded. Confirm the exact conditions in the resolution before building on it.

## Digital services from abroad
<!-- es: IVA en servicios digitales del exterior, Netflix, Spotify, plataformas, compras con tarjeta, percepción del IVA por emisores de tarjetas, formularios 155 156 158 -->

Purchases of digital services from foreign platforms by card are subject to IVA through **percepción** by card issuers (TRIBU-CR form 155) or collected by registered foreign providers and intermediaries (forms 158 and 156). See [[tribu-cr]].
