# Simplified tax regime (régimen de tributación simplificada)

## What the law says
<!-- es: régimen de tributación simplificada, acceso voluntario, declaración trimestral, no puede usar crédito fiscal del IVA, no emite factura en algunos casos, artículos 35 a 43 -->

- **Art. 35:** the tax administration "podrá establecer regímenes de tributación simplificada de acceso voluntario" for small taxpayers in specific activities. The regulation sets which activities qualify and the income and purchase limits.
- **Art. 38:** returns are filed **quarterly**, "dentro de los primeros quince días naturales siguientes al trimestre respectivo".
- **Art. 40:** in some cases these taxpayers do not have to issue invoices, but must give a receipt when the buyer asks. Buyers who need a deductible document issue a **factura electrónica de compra** (see [[facturacion-electronica-v44]]).
- **Art. 41:** simplified-regime taxpayers **cannot use IVA input credits**.
- **Art. 4:** they are IVA taxpayers.

## Forms in TRIBU-CR
<!-- es: formularios del régimen simplificado en TRIBU-CR, 104, 105, 153, antes D-105 -->

The old combined D-105 was split into **104** (ISU, individual), **105** (ISU, legal entity) and **153** (IVA, simplified regime), all quarterly.

## Fit for Fact3 clients
<!-- es: quién puede entrar al régimen simplificado, freelancers, desarrolladores, profesionales, exportadores no les conviene, actividades permitidas, límites -->

The regime targets small retail and similar activities listed in the regulation (recent additions included pastry making, succulent sales and costume jewelry, per El Observador). Professional services such as software development and freelancers who export services are generally **not** eligible, and exporters gain nothing from it because they lose the input credit. Default assumption for Fact3: clients are in the general regime (form 150 monthly, 101 annually) unless the accountant confirms otherwise. Pull the current list of eligible activities and limits from the regulation (pending, see PwC 2025 reform).
